01 — Practice

Two disciplines, one roof

Environmental engineering and information technology are usually kept apart. We think that is a mistake: every monitoring program, every compliance report and every water asset eventually becomes a data problem.

  • 01

    Environmental & civil

    Water management systems, sustainable civil practice, and environmental assessment grounded in current study and site experience.

  • 02

    Data & reporting

    Relational data modelling, SQL, and Python analysis built to be re-run — not rebuilt — every reporting cycle.

  • 03

    Platforms & infrastructure

    Linux hosts, automation, and cloud deployment. The unglamorous layer that decides whether a system survives contact with reality.

  • 04

    Public-interest engineering

    Student outreach and community work through Engineers For The Earth, supporting the next cohort into the profession.

03 — How we work

Three commitments

Plain answers

If a system is fragile, we say so — with the evidence, and with what it would take to fix it. No confidence we haven't earned.

Evidence over assertion

Numbers get checked, assumptions get written down, and a result is not a result until it can be reproduced by someone else.

Built to be maintained

Documented, version-controlled, and handed over in a state the next person can pick up. Engineering that only works while you're standing there isn't engineering.
